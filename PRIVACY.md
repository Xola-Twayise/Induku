# Induku privacy policy

_Last updated: 5 October 2026_

Induku is a game that runs entirely on your device.

- **We collect no personal data.** The game has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it does not send anything to any server.
- **On-device settings only.** The game saves two settings on your device: your chosen CPU difficulty and whether sound is on. They never leave your device, and uninstalling the game removes them.
- **Permissions.** On Android the game asks for vibration only, for a short buzz when your fighter is hit. It does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.
- **Children.** Because the game collects no data, it collects none from children either.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
